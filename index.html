How it works

Try. Measure. Revise.

Inhabitant connects a language model to a pretrained motion generator and a scene simulator through two interfaces: spatial control over the motion, and quantitative feedback on its execution. Nothing is trained.

The benchmark, Inhabit

75 long-horizon tasks, 31 layouts.

Each task is one instruction with up to three goals, carried out by the whole body in one of 31 furniture layouts. A task takes 15 to 45 seconds of continuous motion, built from 4 to 12 segments.

Results

74 of 75 tasks completed.

With the same interfaces, the success of Inhabitant follows the language model that acts as the agent. No model is trained.
